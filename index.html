Research Adventures




I am a incoming EE PhD student and graduate research assistant in the applied electromagnetics lab at Colorado State University. I recently graduated summa cum laude with departmental honors from the University of Oregon with a BS in Physics. I was involved in several interdisciplinary research projects from air quality data analysis with UO Physics and Public Policy, to computational glaciology research, to MRI RF coil design at NIST. I hope to always keep learning and finding new ways to apply physics to solving problems across all disciplines. Outside of physics I studied abroad in Taipei Taiwan at National Taiwan Normal University and earned a minor and fluency in Mandarin Chinese. I also completed a minor in mathematics during my BS. While at the UO I competed for the UO club track team racing all distances from the 800m to the marathon at the collegiate level. I hope to continue improving my running, while enjoying the friendships and bonds that running has given to me. Outside of running, I also love to hike, bake, play board games when I can, and (sometimes) cook.
